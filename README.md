# Amadeo

Trader & developer focused on market analysis, Pine Script indicators and trading automation.

## TradingView indicators

- **[[ad] Naked POC](https://github.com/amadeo987/tradingview-naked-poc-dwm)**: untouched daily, weekly and monthly Points of Control.
- **[[ad] Price Action](https://github.com/amadeo987/tradingview-price-action)**: market structure, order blocks, FVG, SFP, VWAP and EMAs.
- **[[ad] Opens & Previous Day H/L](https://github.com/amadeo987/tradingview-opens-pdh-pdl)**: yearly, monthly, weekly and daily opens with previous highs and lows.
- **[[ad] Liquidation Heatmap](https://www.tradingview.com/script/q3vSyjnG-ad-Liquidation-Heatmap/)**: estimated liquidation areas as a heatmap behind price (on TradingView).
